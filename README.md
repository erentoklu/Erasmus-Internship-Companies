# 🌍 Erasmus Internship & Job Search Engine

**🔗 Live Demo:** [internerasmus.com](https://internerasmus.com/)

## 📌 About The Project
Finding an Erasmus internship is notoriously difficult. Most students have to cold-email over 100 companies just to get a handful of replies, and simply finding companies that match specific criteria takes an enormous amount of time. 

I built this project to solve that exact problem. This website serves as a dedicated search engine and database—presented as an interactive table—that helps students and professionals find Erasmus internships and job opportunities much faster. By filtering the database based on your specific criteria, you can bypass the tedious research phase, save countless hours, and focus directly on applying to the right companies.

## ✨ Key Features
* **Interactive Database:** A comprehensive, Excel-style interactive table containing company data.
* **Advanced Filtering:** Easily filter opportunities by specific criteria to match your exact needs and qualifications.
* **Time-Saving:** Drastically reduces the hours spent scouring the web for eligible companies.
* **Multi-Purpose:** Built initially for Erasmus+ mobility students, but equally highly effective for finding general job opportunities.
