# 🚀 Why This Project Exists

Finding an Erasmus internship is far more difficult than it should be.

Most students end up sending **100–300 cold emails** just to receive a handful of replies. Before they can even apply, they spend countless hours researching companies, opening hundreds of websites, and trying to determine which ones match their interests.

I experienced the same problem.

That's why I built this project.

Instead of spending days searching for companies, you can instantly explore a continuously growing database of European companies and filter them based on your own criteria.

Whether you're searching for an **Erasmus internship**, a **regular internship**, or even **full-time job opportunities**, this platform helps you discover relevant companies significantly faster.

# 📈 How the Database Is Built

The database is continuously growing.

Most companies are collected using custom-built scraping tools. Every record is then manually reviewed, cleaned, and organized before being published on the website.

The database is updated weekly and continuously improved through user feedback and regular maintenance.
